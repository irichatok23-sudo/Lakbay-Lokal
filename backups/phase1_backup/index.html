About Lakbay Lokal

Community-Driven Tourism in Biliran Province

Biliran is one of the smallest and most ecologically diverse island provinces in the Philippines. From the volcanic peaks of Mt. Biliran to the shifting sandbars of Higatangan and the crystal waters of Sambawan, our mission is sustainable, community-first preservation.

Direct Livelihood Retention

100% of guide fees, boat charters, and culinary packages go directly to local barangay families without middleman deductions.

Ecological Carrying Capacity

Real-time footfall logging by Barangay Officials ensures marine sanctuaries and waterfalls are never overcrowded.

Verified & Accredited Safety

All service providers undergo Barangay and DOT validation, providing tourists with safe and reliable travel experiences.
